# Category templates

Fill only what is true for the product. Ask the user for missing facts.

## Apparel

- Title: `Brand + garment + fabric/fit + gender/age + colour`
- Bullets: fabric feel and weight (gsm), fit and length, stretch, details (pockets, collar),
  care, occasion
- Specs: fabric composition %, size chart (cm), model height and size worn
- Objections: size exchange policy, colour may vary slightly by screen

## Electronics and accessories

- Title: `Brand + product + model + key spec (capacity/power/size) + compatibility`
- Bullets: main job and result, battery life/charge time, compatibility list, ports, durability,
  what is in the box
- Specs: model number, dimensions, weight, capacity (mAh/GB), input/output (V/W), warranty
- Objections: official warranty vs seller warranty, authenticity, return for defects

## Beauty and personal care

- Title: `Brand + product + key ingredient/benefit + skin/hair type + size`
- Bullets: who it is for, how it feels, key ingredients and what they do, how to use, results
  timeline (only if the brand states it)
- Specs: volume (ml/g), full ingredient list, shelf life after opening, origin
- Objections: patch test advice, authenticity, expiry date shown on delivery
- Never: medical claims ("cures acne") or invented test results

## Food and grocery

- Title: `Brand + product + variety/origin + weight/pack`
- Bullets: taste/use, origin and harvest, processing, storage, serving ideas
- Specs: net weight, ingredients, allergens, shelf life, storage temperature, certifications
  (only with proof)
- Objections: delivery packaging, freshness guarantee

## Furniture and home

- Title: `Brand + item + material + size/seats + colour`
- Bullets: where it fits, comfort/use, material and finish, assembly time, care
- Specs: W x D x H (cm), weight, max load, materials, assembly required, warranty
- Objections: delivery and assembly service, measuring doorways, returns for large items

## Handmade and artisan (e.g. jamdani, nakshi kantha, pottery)

- Title: `Product + craft/technique + material + origin + colour`
- Bullets: who made it and how, time to make, material, feel, care, uniqueness ("slight
  variations are part of handmade work")
- Specs: dimensions, weight, material, care
- Objections: colour variation, delivery time for made-to-order pieces
